Dungeon Bastards

Steal weird shit. Betray your crew. Die ugly.

An online co-op dungeon crawl for a crew of bastards who don't trust each other. Break into the dungeon as a crew, fight through monsters, and grab every cursed trinket you can carry. Then race for the lift. It has one seat fewer than the crew, and only loot you carry out counts.

Friendly fire is on. Pick your crew's pockets, leave them bleeding out, or drag them back up if you still need them. Die and you come back as a ghost; kill someone living and their body is yours. Whatever you stash in your locker comes down to the next floor, deeper and nastier each time.

  • Online co-op: start a crew and send your friends the 4-letter code or invite link
  • No install, no account: runs in the browser, and the host's tab runs the dungeon
  • Any device with Chrome: PC, Mac, laptop or phone, with keyboard & mouse, controller, or touch
  • Three bastards to choose from once you're in the crew: the Rat, the Kangaroo and the Goblin
