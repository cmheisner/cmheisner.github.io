Dungeon Bastards

Room

    Friendly fire is always on. Loot only counts if you carry it out the EXIT.
    Screwing your crew over for your own gain (stealing, snatching bodies, baiting monsters, dirty tricks, fake gifts) fills your Bastard meter. Plain fighting doesn't count. Full meter: press Q to go full bastard.
    Go down and your crew has 12 seconds to pick you up (and you'll owe them). Die and you come back as a ghost. Kill a living bastard as a ghost and you steal their body.
    The lift has one seat fewer than your crew. Ride it together for a bonus. Escape without hurting anyone for another.
    Everyone has a vice and a secret contract. Rats fit through vents, goblins blow vaults, kangaroos kick boulders. The exit door takes two.
    Stash gold, cursed loot, potions or scrolls in a locker: if anybody gets out, the crew goes a floor deeper and your locker comes too. Crewmates can crack it open, loudly. Rig it.
    Spells cost mana and can fizzle (less as you level). Grab tomes to learn more. Scrolls never fizzle. Potions are potions. Gutter Sugar is a mistake.
